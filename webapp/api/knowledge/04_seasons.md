# Seasons and confounders (North-Western Zambia)

- **Wet season: about Nov–Apr.** Frequent cloud. Vegetation greens up from Dec to Mar. Water bodies and dambos fill. Bare areas look smaller because grass covers their edges.
- **Dry season: about May–Oct.** Clear skies. Grass dries out; miombo trees lose leaves from around Aug–Oct. Water levels fall to a minimum around Oct–Nov.
- **Fire: about Jun–Oct, peaking Aug–Sep.** Early and late dry-season burning of grassland and woodland is common. Burn scars look dark (and slightly reddish) in RGB. Most burning is outside the mine.
- **Haze and smoke** in the late dry season raise blue reflectance and lower contrast.
- **What this means for change.**
  - The assignment pair spans early July to late Aug / early Sep 2023, in the dry season. Grass drying, leaf drop and burning cause change outside the mine that is not mining.
  - To measure mine growth, compare dates in the **same season** (e.g. dry season to dry season); otherwise vegetation differences dominate.
  - Darkening along drainage lines in Aug–Sep is usually fire or wetland dynamics, not mining.
