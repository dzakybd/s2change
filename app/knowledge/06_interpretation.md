# Interpretation playbook

| Signal | Most likely meaning | Check with |
|---|---|---|
| Brightening + redder (Δred ≫ Δblue) on a waste dump | Fresh, oxidized waste rock tipped | A later date: does the dump keep growing? |
| Bare area appearing at a pit rim | Pit widening (pushback) | A later same-season date; haul roads extending |
| Bright/dark patches moving on the TSF | Tailings deposition moving between spigot points | Later dates; the TSF outline should stay stable |
| Bright arc along the TSF wall | Earthworks or vegetation stripped for maintenance (no raise is on record for 2013 to 2027) | VHR imagery; the operator's maintenance and construction log |
| TSF pond area changing sharply | Water management change (or, if unexplained, worth an inspection) | Water rule on both dates; adjacent wetland changes |
| Darkening outside the footprint, Aug–Sep | Burn scar or wetland dynamics | Active-fire records; recovery after rains |
| Dam surface darker and bluer | More open water / less floating vegetation, or a turbidity change | Shoreline position on a later date |
| New bare area outside the known footprint, persisting | Possible new clearing or expansion; the highest-priority lead | VHR imagery, licence boundaries |
| Changes along straight lines matching the mosaic seams | Artifact | Compare against the source-scene map |

**Priority for a monitoring brief:**
1. New disturbance outside the footprint.
2. Facility growth (pits, dumps).
3. TSF and water anomalies.
4. Seasonal noise. Mention it only to explain why it was excluded.

**Land cover before → after of the changed area** (default comparison, simple RGB classes from `rgb.cover`; a reading aid, not a land-cover map): bare → bare (re-worked ground) 1.22 km², 30 %; bare → bright surface (fresh tailings, graded ground) 0.75 km², 18 %; vegetation → bare (new disturbance) 0.62 km², 15 %, of which 51 % is the tailings dam-wall fill and 23 % waste dumping; bright → bare (tailings drying or covered) 0.54 km², 13 %; water → water (pond colour, not extent) 0.40 km², 10 %; bare → water (ponding) 0.18 km², 4 %; other 0.42 km², 10 %.
