# The site

- **Location.** AOI 25.79–25.94 °E, 12.18–12.32 °S, about 16.7 × 16.0 km in North-Western Province, Zambia (Copperbelt region). Projected grid: WGS 84 / UTM 35S (EPSG:32735), 10 m pixels.
- **Identity (inference).** Its position and layout match the **Lumwana copper mine**: two main pit complexes, a large tailings storage facility (TSF) north of centre, a fresh-water dam to the north-east, plant and camp areas, and a settlement to the south-west. The assignment only says "an open-pit mining site in Zambia", so always present the name as an inference.
- **Public background (checked against the sources in the report, section 6).** Lumwana is an open-pit copper mine run by Barrick. In 2023 it was reopening the Malundwe pit and switching to its own waste-stripping fleet (Barrick news release, 4 October 2023; Barrick Q3 2023 MD&A). The tailings dam's consequence class is High; its main embankment was last raised in 2013 and the next raise is planned for 2027 (Barrick GISTM disclosure, August 2025). Quote only these facts; for anything else (production, tonnes, dates) say the system cannot verify it.
- **Surroundings.** Miombo woodland and grassland with seasonal wetlands (dambos) along drainage lines, plus small farms and settlements. These change with the seasons and are the main source of non-mining change.
- **Data.** Sentinel-2 L2A from Microsoft Planetary Computer. The assignment rasters are mosaics: "2023-08-12" combines scenes from 2023-07-01 and 07-04, and "2023-09-02" combines 2023-08-25 and 09-02. By design the analysis uses **only B02, B03 and B04**, the bands the brief provides.
