# Facility classes (what they are, how they look at 10 m)

| Class | What it is | RGB signature (B02/B03/B04 only) | Typical change |
|---|---|---|---|
| `open_pit` | Excavation where ore and waste are removed in benches | Bare, terraced benches (high texture), darker shadowed walls, sometimes a pit lake (blue > red); shadows vary with sun angle | Widening at the rim, ramps moving, pit lakes appearing or disappearing |
| `waste_dump` | Rock removed to reach the ore, placed in mounds near pits | Bare, red/orange (iron oxides: high redness B04/B02), smooth tipped faces | Grows outward or upward as new lifts are tipped; fresh faces brighter and redder |
| `tailings_beach` | Fine residue from processing, deposited as slurry in the TSF | Very bright, smooth, flat; wet areas darker | Wet/dry patches move as deposition points change; the beach extends over time |
| `tailings_pond` | Free water ponding on the TSF | Water: blue clearly above red, not bright; turquoise to dark | Shrinks in the dry season, expands in the wet season; sudden changes are worth checking |
| `water_reservoir` / `process_pond` | Fresh-water dam, process and sediment ponds | Water rule (blue > red, not bright) in both dates | Seasonal level changes; turbidity or algae changes colour |
| `plant_infrastructure` | Processing plant, workshops, camp, stockpile pads | Small, compact, bright and high contrast | New pads or buildings |
| `haul_road` | Roads connecting pits, dumps and plant | Long, narrow, bright | New roads point to new working areas |
| `other_disturbed` | Bare ground that fits no class | Bare | Clearing, laydown areas, borrow pits |

**Embankment raise.** The TSF wall is raised in stages. New fill, or vegetation stripped from the wall face, appears as a bright arc along the crest.
